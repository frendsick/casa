Risk-adjusted prototype read: Compiler Capsule is the lead to challenge. It removes the public operation, store, bytecode, program, and editor-query protocols while reusing the most current implementation. Function-at-a-Time Direct Compiler is the strongest radical challenger if a vertical slice proves body indexing, generic recipes, and partial editor results. Typed Product Ladder is the clearest phase model, but its interface and memory costs need stronger justification. This is a recommendation for discussion, not the target decision.
